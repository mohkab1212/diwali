# Shubh Deepavali

A Diwali app from Mohit and Vartika for friends and family: the story, the five days, puja at home, the mantras, songs, greetings to send, fireworks, cards, party games, recipes and rangoli.

Open it at https://mohkab1212.github.io/diwali/

On a phone, add it to the home screen from the browser's share menu and it opens like an app.
